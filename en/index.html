WHAT WE DO FOR YOU

Services

We adapt the service to your home, its location and the time you spend away.

⌂

Home supervision

We check the interior and exterior, look for visible signs of damp, leaks or other issues, and ventilate when appropriate.

⌕

Issue management

If something needs attention, we let you know and help you consider next steps. We coordinate professionals when needed and with your approval.

⌁

Basic home care

During visits, we can take care of small tasks such as ventilation, watering plants or checking outdoor areas, according to your home and service plan.

▤

Photo reports

You receive photographs and a clear summary of your home’s condition, including relevant observations and any issues that may need attention.

♙

Personal attention

We adapt visits to your home’s particular needs and priorities, with direct, attentive and transparent communication.
