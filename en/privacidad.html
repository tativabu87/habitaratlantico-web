Privacy Policy

HabitarAtlántico takes the privacy of people who contact us seriously. This page explains how we handle data submitted through the contact form.

1. Data controller

Controller: HabitarAtlántico

Contact: info@habitaratlantico.es

Note: before this policy is published as a final legal notice, the legal identity, tax ID and registered address of the controller should be completed and the terms applicable to the activity reviewed.

2. Data we may collect

When you use the contact form, we may receive your name, email address, phone number, home location and any information included in your message.

3. How we use the data

We use the data to respond to your enquiry, understand your home’s needs and, where appropriate, prepare a service proposal.

4. Retention

We retain data for as long as necessary to manage your enquiry and while related liabilities may arise, and thereafter for the applicable periods.

Form submissions are processed through Formspree, the service used to receive and manage form submissions. Personal data is not sold.

6. Your rights

You may request access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction or portability where applicable. To exercise your rights, email info@habitaratlantico.es.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated when the activity, services used or applicable legal requirements change.
