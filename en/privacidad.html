LEGAL INFORMATION

Privacy Policy

This draft explains how personal data submitted through the HabitarAtlántico website would be handled. It must be checked against the site's actual operation before being published as a final version.

DRAFT PENDING LEGAL REVIEW — DO NOT PUBLISH AS A FINAL VERSION. The controller's full legal identity and required address remain to be confirmed. No private home address or tax ID is included.

Draft last updated: 21 October 2026.

1. Data controller

Controller: HabitarAtlántico — legal identity to be completed before publication.

Privacy contact: info@habitaratlantico.es.

2. Data we may collect

The contact form requests a name, email address and the location of the home; it also allows users to provide a phone number, service type and message. Please do not include unnecessary personal data or sensitive information in the message.

The form provider may process technical data associated with a submission, such as an IP address, depending on its operation and configuration. This must be checked against the provider account and terms.

3. Purposes and legal basis

Data would be used to respond to enquiries, assess a home's needs and, where appropriate, prepare a proposal requested by the interested person. The intended legal basis is taking steps at the person's request before entering into a contract and/or legitimate interest in handling communications, depending on the specific case. The applicable basis must be confirmed for each processing activity.

Data should not be used to send unsolicited marketing. Any additional use requires an appropriate legal basis and, where applicable, separate consent.

4. Service providers and recipients

Contact form submissions are handled through Formspree. Before publication, the provider's terms, its role in processing, retention periods, security measures and any international data transfers must be reviewed. Personal data is not sold.

5. Retention

Data will be kept only for as long as necessary to respond to and manage the enquiry. If no service relationship begins, it will be deleted or restricted when no longer needed, unless it must be retained for legally required periods. Specific periods must be defined to reflect the actual activity.

6. Website analytics and cookies

The proposed website implementation contemplates Google Analytics 4 for usage statistics. Under that proposal, optional analytics would load only after express acceptance, and the choice could be rejected or changed. This description is accurate only if the integration and consent mechanism are correctly tested before publication. If Analytics is not active on the public website, it must not be described as processing currently taking place.

Before analytics is activated, the Google Analytics configuration, retention periods, information about international transfers and applicable cookie policy must be checked.

7. Your rights

Where applicable, you may request access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction of processing and data portability, and withdraw consent without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing. Contact info@habitaratlantico.es. You may also lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD).

8. Security

Appropriate technical and organisational measures should be applied to protect personal data and restrict access to people who need it to manage the enquiry.

9. Changes

This policy will be updated if processing activities, providers or legal requirements change. The published version must show the date of its actual latest update.
