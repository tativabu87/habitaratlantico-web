2. Data we may collect

The contact form requests a name, email address and the location of the home; it also allows users to provide a phone number, service type and message. Please do not include unnecessary personal data or sensitive information in the message.

Formspree processes form submissions to provide the contact-form service. Technical data may also be processed as described in Formspree’s applicable privacy information.

3. Purposes and legal basis

Personal data is processed to respond to enquiries, assess a home's needs and, where appropriate, prepare a proposal requested by the interested person. The legal basis is taking steps at the person's request before entering into a contract, where applicable.

Data should not be used to send unsolicited marketing. Any additional use requires an appropriate legal basis and, where applicable, separate consent.

4. Service providers and recipients

Contact-form submissions are handled through Formspree, which processes data to provide the form service. Data may be shared with service providers strictly as needed to operate the website and respond to enquiries. Personal data is not sold.

5. Retention

Data will be kept for as long as necessary to respond to and manage the enquiry and, where applicable, for legally required retention periods. It will be deleted or restricted when it is no longer needed for the purpose for which it was collected, unless retention is required by law.

6. Website analytics and cookies

The website may use Google Analytics 4 to produce usage statistics only if the visitor expressly accepts analytics cookies. If analytics is rejected, it remains disabled. The choice can be changed later using the cookie settings option.

Google Analytics is a service provided by Google. Analytics is activated only after the relevant consent and uses the available settings configured for the service.

7. Your rights

Where applicable, you may request access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction of processing and data portability, and withdraw consent without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing. Contact info@habitaratlantico.es. You may also lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD).

8. Security

Appropriate technical and organisational measures are used to protect personal data and restrict access to people who need it to manage the enquiry.

9. Changes

This policy will be updated if processing activities, providers or legal requirements change. The published version must show the date of its actual latest update.
